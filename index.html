{About me}

Who I Am and How I Got Here.

I’m a UI/UX Engineer and No-Code Developer with nearly three years of freelance experience — turning ideas into digital products that feel simple and human. My journey began designing flyers and mobile visuals in college, where I discovered how design can shape connection. That curiosity grew into a focus on product thinking, refined through self-learning, UX courses, and hands-on projects that built my foundation in creating meaningful user experiences.

Over time, I’ve worked across industries like community management, cloud computing, and data analytics — bridging design and development to deliver scalable, purposeful solutions. My process is grounded in research, clarity, and collaboration, helping businesses translate complex ideas into intuitive design outcomes. Outside of work, I draw inspiration from music and technology — both remind me that creativity and structure can harmonize beautifully.
