a little about me.

I am Boluwatife, a UI/UX Designer and Frontend Engineer and I believe the world is nothing without intentional design. I get butterflies seeing a concept come to life and I started off with visual design as a hobby and now I've spent over three years creating human-centric designs and bringing ideas to life.

I have been privileged to work with diverse teams in different industries from data consultancy, healthcare, real estate and others, and I use both design, development and other skills to turn complex ideas into human-centric products. My goal is to solve problems with clean design that helps businesses communicate value and reach profitable outcomes. I put unnecessary care into every detail because I believe people should feel someone made this with them in mind.
